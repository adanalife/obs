Three new manifest invariants cover the album bed's music share: it mounts at the path `script/background-audio.sh` actually scans (read from the script, so the two cannot drift), it mounts read-only on both the volumeMount and the claim, and every prod OBS claims the `prod-stream` priority class. Each was a rule stated only in a comment; a break in any of them degrades or drops a live stream rather than failing a deploy.
