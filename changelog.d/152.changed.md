Twitch now starts on the licensed album bed like every other platform — no platform defaults to SomaFM anymore. SomaFM stays fully selectable from the console, `!somafm` and `OBS_BACKGROUND_AUDIO`; it is just no longer where a stream boots, since its edges have refused our IP and a boot that starts there can start on a bed that never plays. The carhum drone remains the automatic fallback when the music share has no tracks.
