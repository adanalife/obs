TikTok now streams at 30fps (was 60): TikTok recommends 30fps for non-gaming content and its portrait LIVE is unstable at 60. Framerate is now overridable via `OBS_FPS_COMMON` independent of the quality preset, so TikTok keeps the high preset's 1080p resolution + bitrate at 30fps (a new per-platform `obs_fps` cdk8s override, mirroring `obs_video_bitrate_kbps`).
