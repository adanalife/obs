The browser-source refresher now reloads only sources that are actually dead. Every five minutes it screenshots each browser source and, for any showing a blank frame, swaps its url through `about:blank` and back to respawn the CEF renderer — the move that recovered the crashed overlays by hand. Sources with content on them are left alone, so the hourly unconditional `refreshnocache` (which could not revive a crashed page and once appeared to trigger a crash) is gone.
